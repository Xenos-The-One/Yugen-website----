## Reviews (target: 25+ in 90 days)

Use Raindrop's own review system on Raindrop's own clients. That's the best proof the product works.

- Ask every active and past client personally first (a text from the founder converts best), then automate it after every milestone (site launch, first ranking win, first month report).
- Ask them to mention the **service** and **city** naturally ("Raindrop rebuilt our HVAC website in Vaughan…"). Never script the review.
- Reply to every review within 48 hours, using the client's service and city in the reply.
- **Never** offer anything in exchange for a review, review your own business, or post reviews written by staff. Google removes them, and Canada's Competition Bureau treats fake or incentivized reviews as deceptive marketing.

Review request text (send from your phone):

> Hi {first name}, it's Thailer from Raindrop. Really glad we got {result, e.g. "the new site live"} for you. If you have 30 seconds, a Google review would mean a lot to a small business like ours: {review link}. Thank you!
