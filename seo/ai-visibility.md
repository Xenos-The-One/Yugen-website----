## Google rank tracking ($0)

Each month, export Search Console → Performance → Queries (last 28 days) and compare against `keyword-map.csv`:

- **Position 1–10:** protect it. Refresh the content yearly and keep internal links pointing at it.
- **Position 8–20:** the biggest opportunity. Expand the page, add internal links, earn 1–2 links to it.
- **High impressions, low CTR:** rewrite the title and description.
- **Not showing at all:** check indexing (URL Inspection) and whether the page really targets the term.
